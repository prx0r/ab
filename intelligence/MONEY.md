## Calibrations for us

1. **Price band proven: £1–3k/yr per tradie.** Checkatrade extracts ~£2k/yr for
   shared leads alone. Our retainer (£99–249/mo = £1.2–3k/yr) for leads +
   answering + stack sits inside demonstrated willingness-to-pay. Never
   underprice to £69/mo — that's the commoditized slice.
2. **Take rate proven: GTV is the prize.** ServiceTitan's $68.5B GTV at
   ~1.3% implied take (~$900M rev) shows the OS earns on flow, not seats.
   Our 15–20% per-job spread + payout float is the same shape at sole-trader
   scale. Money-in-the-loop > SaaS seats.
3. **Boring prints money.** Checkatrade's PRINTED directory did £19.6M in
   2024. Unsexy distribution to older homeowners works. Our equivalents:
   landlord letters, EICR piggyback, parish-mag ads. Do not sneer at paper.
4. **Their margin is our opening.** 2.5% op margin on 555 staff = humans doing
   what agents do. An agent-operated competitor needs a fraction of the
   headcount per £1M revenue. That gap is the whole game.
5. **Nobody owns UK sole-trader EV depth.** All proven money is either US FSM,
   generic UK leads, or the vans themselves. The grant-shaped, OZEV-fluent,
   aftercare-attached UK installer stack has no revenue line anywhere yet —
   which is exactly why ab-001 exists.
