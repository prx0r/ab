## The 5 alpha queries (SIC 43210 = electrical installation)

1. **New 43210 incorporations by postcode × month** → recruit list + supply
   velocity. This is d(scarcity)/dt from the aithesis, measured directly.
2. **Dissolutions in 43210/43220** → retirement/churn map; orphaned customer
   bases (warranty-flip targets) cluster where firms die.
3. **Officers + charges** → serial founders, growth signals (who's borrowing
   for vans/stock), director age bands.
4. **Competitor accounts watch** → repeat the Vetted play annually; add
   MyBuilder/Rated People/Bark entities + regional challengers. Revenue,
   margins, member counts, dividends — free.
5. **Landlord funnel (SIC 68209, 68320)** → newly formed property/letting
   firms per cluster = B2B outreach list for the landlord bundle.

## Honest caveat (load-bearing)

**Companies House covers Ltd companies only.** The majority of 1-man-band
sparkies are sole traders — invisible here. CH is the *formal-economy* lens:
growing firms, switchers incorporating, churn, competitors, landlords. Pair
with Places (everyone with a listing) + NICEIC (the competent) for full
coverage. Three lenses, one list.
