# SUPPLY — training-as-onboarding (the labor mirror of the aithesis)

*Date: 2026-09-10. Sources: ECA Skills Index 2026, JTL/CITB, C&G+Shell programme,
Skills England AU0006, provider pricing. Maps to `drop/aithesis.md` §§5,7,17,24,29.*

## The thesis in one line

AI eats revenue-per-friction desk work (aithesis §29) → displaced workers must
flow into irreducible physical work (§24) → electrical is the highest-paid,
most-permissioned trade in the electrification wave → **the pipeline converting
interest into qualified sparkies is broken, and whoever owns the conversion
owns the supply.**

## The shortage (demand for labor, all 2026-fresh)

- 136k qualified electricians, workforce **down 19.6% since 2018**; 12,000 new
  qualifiers/yr needed, 5,000/yr just to stand still (ECA, JTL/CITB).
- Apprenticeship starts **fell 5.5%** while demand surges; 37% of firms cite
  shortage as #1 growth blocker, 12 quarters running.
- Heat pumps: 9k MCS installers vs 150k needed; gas-engineer average age 45.4,
  exodus coming; only 20–25% of heating installers touch heat pumps.
- **The killer stat (ECA 2026 Skills Index): <1 in 5 classroom learners ever
  reach apprenticeship or skilled employment.** Training exists. Jobs exist.
  The bridge doesn't. The bridge is the business.

## The cert ladder (permission scarcity, aithesis §5)

```text
adult: L2+L3 diplomas + NVQ + 18th (2382) + 2391 testing = 18–24 mo (~£3–8k)
  → qualified electrician (ECS/JIB card)
  → C&G 2921-34 EV charging = 2 DAYS (~£415–498) → install immediately
  → extras: solar 2922, battery, heat-pump BPEC, NICEIC approval (needs experience)
```

Two doors: the long door (career changers, 18–24 mo) and the 2-day door
(qualified sparks adding EV). Both leak at conversion. We stand at both exits.

## Others already pay for supply (precedent)

- **GOVERNMENT PAYS:** Skills Bootcamps free for learners incl. self-employed
  (60+ GLH, EV bootcamps live); NEW Skills England unit AU0006 (EV charging,
  35 hrs, £950 funding, approved Mar 2026). Bootcamps are judged on job
  outcomes — providers NEED employers. We are the employer pipeline.
- **CORPORATES PAY:** City & Guilds + Shell UK co-funded 250 EV installers,
  extended +250 more. Supply creation is already a bought market.
- **PROVIDERS BLEED:** Total Skills, TradeSkills4U, JTL graduate adults with
  no supervised hours and no leads. Their outcome stats are our leverage.

## The model: own conversion, invert CAC

```text
desk worker / classroom grad / qualified spark
  → bootcamp or adult diploma (gov/employer-funded, not us)
  → SUPERVISED HOURS via partner sparkies (mate → improver, signed off)
  → EV top-up 2921 (2 days) → graduate onto OUR stack
  → fed jobs + backend + reviews from day one of independence
```

Checkatrade pays Google to acquire tradies. **Our supply acquisition is
funded by government and corporates, and providers compete to send us
graduates because we fix their outcome stats.** Inverted CAC — the funnel
pays us to fill itself.

## Offer per segment

- **Career changers:** the ladder map + supervised-hours guarantee + "jobs
  waiting at the end." Content engine ("spreadsheet to switchboard").
- **Classroom grads (the 4-in-5):** placement as mates with partner firms —
  the missing step ECA can't provide.
- **Qualified sparks:** 2-day EV top-up + immediate job flow ("qualify
  Friday, earning Monday"). Cheapest, most competent supply.
- **Partners (training providers):** outcome stats + employer pipeline; contra
  referral or paid placement.

## Honest caveats (load-bearing)

1. **No shortcutting competence.** ECA is right: bootcamps don't make
   electricians. We own conversion/supervision/flow — never credentials.
   Safety incidents kill the company; csec-grade safety culture extends to
   supervised-hours sign-off.
2. **NICEIC approval needs experience.** Grads start supervised (mate/improver),
   graduate to QS-signed work, then independence. The ladder is 12–24 mo even
   accelerated — sell honestly, retain longer.
3. **Training delivery is NOT phase 1.** Partner, don't build. Classrooms,
   liability, and Ofqual are someone else's capex until ab-001 cash-flows.
   Phase 1 action: ONE partnership conversation (Total Skills/JTL-type) + ONE
   bootcamp provider needing outcomes. Paper before premises.
4. **Three-clock timing (§17):** desk displacement accelerates 2027–29. Build
   the funnel in 2026–27 so it's proven when the wave arrives — early means
   competing for supply; on-time means drowning in it.
