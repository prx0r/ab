# STRATEGY — the smart play (2026-09-10)

## One paragraph

**One cluster, grant-shaped demand, subcontract delivery, leads-first tradie
wedge, stack second, verticals third.** Do not start as a platform. Start as
the best EV installer in one postcode cluster — with a phone that gets
answered and grant paperwork that gets done — then convert the proof (jobs,
reviews, data) into tradie onboarding, then convert the stack into the
business. The grant cliff (31 Mar 2027) subsidises Phase 1 and *creates*
Phase 2: when grants end, tradies' pipelines dry up and our non-grant engine
+ stack is the lifeboat we sell them.

## Why this order (three reasons)

1. **Proof beats pitch.** Tradies are pitched daily by platforms. "Three
   exclusive pre-qualified jobs in MK9 this week — first one's free" opens
   every door; a SaaS demo opens none. Leads are tradie-side CAC at ~£0.
2. **Revenue funds the build.** Phase 1 jobs at ≥£180–250 CM pay for billing,
   ads, Telnyx, OZEV application costs, and the operator's time. No phase
   needs funding from outside itself.
3. **Every job trains the machine.** Photos → identification library. Faults →
   triage graph. Prices → contribution data. Reviews → trust asset. A platform
   launched cold has none of this; a converted installer has all of it.

## The sequence

### Phase 1 — Installer (now → Dec 2026): EvSpark earns
- One cluster (Apify sweep → pick). £10/day exact-match ads. Photo-survey landing.
- Deliver via ONE partner spark on day-rate; we hold customer, scope, grant
  paperwork, aftercare. Never hold the tools ourselves.
- Targets: CAC ≤ £40/lead, close ≥ 20%, CM ≥ £180/job, 8+ jobs, 20+ reviews.
- Milestone that matters: **3 real paid jobs.** Everything else is downstream.

### Phase 2 — Wedge (Jan → Mar 2027): proof becomes supply
- Partner spark becomes tradie #1 on the stack (kernel on their number via
  call-forward like zera, diary, invoices, reviews, WhatsApp).
- Recruit 2–3 more in adjacent postcodes *with job proof in hand*.
- OZEV authorisation lands → grant jobs unlock → CM step-change for the sprint.
- Sell the flip honestly: "grants end March — our engine doesn't need them."

### Phase 3 — Stack (from Apr 2027): the business becomes the machine
- EvSpark-installer is reference customer #1, not the company.
- Revenue: retainers (£99–249/mo) + per-job spread (15–20%) + payout float.
- New verticals cost one YAML + one csec pack extension (sparky already does
  consumer units/faults; flowright proves plumbing). ab-002, ab-003 spin cheap.
- Parts-aftercare wedge (PCB triage, national catchment, no van) per HCC pivot.

## The two clever bits

1. **Piggyback the EICR.** England rentals need EICR every 5 years / change of
   tenancy — an electrician is *already inside the property*. Marginal cost of
   quoting an EV charger ≈ 0. Partner with EICR electricians (they bring
   property access; we bring EV scoping + grant paperwork + aftercare) instead
   of competing with them. This is `RISK_PRICER_SUBSIDIZES_PREVENTION` as a
   sales motion, and EICR due-dates are trigger data we can buy/lists-build.
2. **Landlords-first option.** 200-socket cap per landlord, scheme penetration
   1,912 sockets *ever*, EICR bundle, one sale = many jobs. If consumer CAC
   misbehaves, flip the top of funnel to 10 landlords × 20 sockets and skip
   the homeowner scrum entirely. Keep this as the standing Plan B.

## What NOT to do

- Fight Octopus on tariff (7–8p/kWh integration is their moat). Sell *around*
  it: grant-max, firm pricing, edge segments, aftercare.
- Build dispatch v0 / job ledger / onboarding funnel before 3 paid jobs.
  Code is not the bottleneck; a live job is.
- Hold tools, hold stock, or operate outside Part P scope. Subcontract until
  volume forces the question.
- Build a cost base that needs grants. Every hire/commit must survive April.

## Honest risks

- **Execution bandwidth.** One operator + agents, real vans, real liability.
  Mitigated by subcontract-first and one cluster only.
- **Spark dependency.** If no qualified spark < £250/day in-cluster, delivery
  breaks (PLAN.md kill condition stands — EICR-partner route is the hedge).
- **Platforms copy.** They won't do unsexy per-cluster work (answered phones,
  grant forms, landlord letters). National scale is their strength and their
  blindness.
- **The meta-risk (drop's own verdict): "Stop building infrastructure. Start
  running experiments."** The stack is sufficient. The next milestone is
  revenue, not code.
