# 1. The labor mirror (why now, formally)

The aithesis (§§29/24/17) predicts exactly this market:

- §29: AI attacks **revenue-per-friction** work (admin, support, junior
  analysis, BPO seats). Desk employment degrades first.
- §24: value migrates to **irreducible** observations and actions. A consumer
  unit swap cannot be synthesized, offshored, or prompted.
- §17 (three clocks): capability (2026) → deployment → cashflow (layoffs
  2027–29). **The migration wave is timed, not hypothetical.** Build the
  funnel in 2026–27; be proven when it arrives.

Prediction: **desk workers become sparkies.** Not metaphorically — the
TradeSkills4U taster-day queue already shows it (a social worker retraining
in Warrington, on record). Our machine stands at the exit.

# 2. The broken conversion (the gap, measured)

- Workforce **−25% since 2018** (JTL, ONS-based); 12,000 new qualifiers/yr
  needed; starts must rise 7.5k → 10k/yr just to stand still.
- Apprentice starts **−5.5%** into surging demand; 37% of firms cite shortage
  as the #1 growth blocker, 12 quarters running (ECA).
- **<1 in 5** classroom learners ever reach a job (ECA Skills Index 2026).
- JTL — the biggest provider, 8,200 learners, 3,800 employers — tells
  applicants on its own site: *"It's a tough industry to find work in… you
  will need an employer before you can start."*

Training exists. Jobs exist. **The bridge doesn't.** The bridge is the business.

# 6. The invariant platform (one machine, eight deltas)

Intake → aggregation feed → <60s response → after-hours → booking → comms →
demand → money → shield → controller. Identical for every trade; per-trade
delta is eight content items (services, price book, safety rules, regs, area,
csec extension, brand+number, ad copy). Full spec: `PLATFORM.md`. The unified
feed and calendar adapter are the two real builds; everything else exists.

# 8. Demand generation (cluster math)

One 25km cluster (~300k households): ~1.5k charger jobs/yr ≈ **£1.3M/yr local
install value**. Five sparkies × 2 EV jobs/week clear it, plus faults, EICR
bundles, landlord stock, battery retrofits. CITB: workforce strategy must go
**place-based**. DESNZ/MCS regions pick the clusters. Copy the cluster.
Full playbook: `intelligence/COMPETITION.md`, `businesses/evspark/PLAN.md`.

# 9. Speed-to-lead + plug behind platforms (decided)

Marketplaces sell each lead 3–20×; tradies lose on response time. Kernel
answers <60s, qualified, every time. Don't replace Checkatrade/MyBuilder —
**sit behind them** (lead-alert parsers via cmail), sell "never miss a lead,"
then exclusive EvSpark leads, then the stack. Their shared-lead model
structurally can't copy instant-exclusive response (conflict of interest).
Spec: `intelligence/MARKETPLACES.md`.

# 10. Money in the loop (calibrated)

Proven bands: ~£2k/yr/tradie (Checkatrade £105.9M FY24, profitable, +33%),
$59–349/mo FSM seats (Housecall ~$352M, Jobber 400k pros), ServiceTitan $961M
at ~$90k/customer/yr on $68.5B GTV. Our shape: retainer £99–249/mo (inside
proven willingness-to-pay — never race to £69) + 15–20% per-job spread +
payout float. GTV take > seats. Full record: `intelligence/MONEY.md`.

**Partnership ladder (in order):** ① one bootcamp provider needing outcomes

graduate placement contra (their outcome stats rise; our supply flows) →

(only after ab-001 cash-flows). **No classrooms, no Ofqual, no liability
until revenue says so.** Training delivery is someone else's capex forever
if partnerships hold.

   discipline, payout reliability.)

   (Hard: saying no to geography.)
5. **Vertical depth** — OZEV/Part P/DNO/EICR/MCS encoded, not blogged.
   (Hard: unsexy regulatory grind + csec-grade safety culture.)
